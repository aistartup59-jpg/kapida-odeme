# ADR-013: POS-Only Payment Acceptance

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-31
- **Supersedes:** ADR-004

## Context

The product is built around a POS device carried by a delivery employee. Payment is taken face to face, at the door, on that device. Payment Links were the one accepted method that did not fit this model: the customer receives a URL, leaves the device, and pays elsewhere, at some later time. That flow needed delivery channels (SMS, WhatsApp, Copy Link) whose only purpose was to move a URL to the customer, and it split the product across two very different payment experiences.

## Decision

Payment is accepted on the POS device only.

`PaymentMethod` values:

- `QR` — a real Bank QR (TR Karekod / EMV QR) issued by the merchant's provider and displayed on the device (ADR-003).
- `NFC` — a card read by the device's NFC reader (Android only).
- `CASH` — cash handed to the employee at the door.

Payment Links are removed. `DeliveryChannel` is removed entirely: with no URL to deliver, SMS, WhatsApp and Copy Link have nothing to carry. Neither SMS nor WhatsApp was ever a payment method (that part of ADR-004 still holds), so nothing about payment acceptance is lost by removing them.

Provider dispatch follows from this:

- `QR` is the only method that calls the provider at PaymentRequest creation time, because the bank QR payload must exist before the customer can scan it.
- `NFC` is captured by the device and `CASH` never involves a provider. Both are reported afterwards as Transactions through `POST /payments/:id/transactions`.

`PaymentProvider.createPaymentLink` is removed from the provider interface. `generateBankQR` is the single provider capability the payment flow dispatches to.

## Consequences

- Hybrid payments (ADR-002) are unchanged: one PaymentRequest may still be completed by multiple Transactions — QR + Cash, NFC + Cash, QR + NFC, or any combination of the three.
- The API no longer accepts `deliveryChannel`, and no longer returns `deliveryChannel`, `linkUrl` or `linkExpiresAt`.
- `PAYMENT_LINK` is rejected on new payment requests and new transactions.
- Rows recorded before this decision keep their values exactly as recorded. Financial history is immutable (ADR-012), so no PaymentRequest or Transaction is rewritten to fit the reduced vocabulary: `paymentMethod` became a text column with a CHECK constraint that still permits the retired `PAYMENT_LINK` value, and `deliveryChannel` was renamed to `legacyDeliveryChannel` rather than dropped.
- Customer Secure Mode remains mandatory for NFC PIN entry — removing Payment Links does not weaken it.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keeping Payment Links as a secondary flow: rejected because it keeps the product split between an at-the-door experience and a pay-later experience, and drags delivery channels, link expiry and link-state handling along with it.
- Removing `CASH` as well: rejected. Cash at the door is the original product scenario, and it composes with QR and NFC through hybrid payments.
- Rewriting historical `PAYMENT_LINK` rows to another method: rejected outright — it would falsify financial history and violate ADR-012.
